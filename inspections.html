Questions about
vehicle inspections

Which inspection should I choose?

Tell us whether you’re buying, selling or checking a vehicle you already own. Mention any particular concerns, and we’ll help you choose an appropriate inspection.

What will the inspection cover?

The checks depend on the vehicle, the access available and what you need us to investigate. We’ll agree what is included and how we’ll share the findings before you book.

Can an inspection guarantee there are no faults?

No inspection can uncover every hidden fault or guarantee future reliability. Our findings relate to the areas we can access and check at the time.
